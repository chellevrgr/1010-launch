I.

Bazi Natal Chart Reading 八字

Understand Your Personal Chart and Life Patterns

In person or remote

Your birth date and time contain more information than most people realize. Bazi extracts that data as eight characters and uses them to map how you are built: your temperament, your pressure points, where your energy concentrates and where it leaks. It also maps timing. Luck Pillars shift roughly every ten years. The one you are in right now is shaping what is open and what is closed for you.

A session covers your chart, your elemental profile, and your current Luck Pillar. Bring a question if you have one. Career, finances, relationships, a decision you cannot see clearly. It becomes part of the reading.

After the session, you receive copies of your Bazi charts and the presentation slides used during the consultation.

Zoom by default. In-person in Makati on request. You will need your birth date, time, and place of birth. Birth time is important for a complete chart, so if yours is uncertain, let Kesler know when you book.

II.

Feng Shui 風水

Understanding the Environment Around You

In-person only (Metro Manila). Never virtual.

Every property has a relationship with its environment. The road in front of it, the structures beside it, the direction it faces, how the interior is arranged. Feng Shui reads those conditions systematically and identifies what they mean for the people using the space.

A generic tip from the internet cannot do that. The same recommendation that works for one property can be wrong for another. The assessment has to come from the actual space.

Kesler conducts all Feng Shui work in person. During the visit, he takes compass measurements, reviews the floor plan, and examines the external environment and internal configuration. The analysis covers both the physical setup and the traditional Feng Shui calculations applied to that specific property.

You receive a written report after the visit with the findings and recommendations. Recommendations are grounded in what the space actually allows, not what would be ideal in a vacuum.

Available within Metro Manila and surrounding areas. Other locations by prior arrangement. You will need to provide the property address, any available floor plans or layout sketches, and information about how the space is used.

III.

Date Selection 擇日

Choosing Suitable Timing for Important Activities

Remote-friendly

Timing matters. Not every date works for every activity, and not every auspicious day on the Chinese calendar is suitable for your specific event and the people involved. Date Selection works through those variables systematically.

Kesler evaluates potential dates using established Chinese calendrical methods alongside the personal details of the individuals involved. The assessment considers the nature of the event, the interactions between the date and the people, and the practical constraints you are working within. The goal is to find options that hold up on both the traditional and the logistical side.

Commonly requested for weddings and family occasions, business openings and launches, office and residential move-ins, groundbreaking ceremonies, and contract signings.

You will need to provide the nature of the event, your preferred scheduling window, the birth information of the principal people involved, and any hard scheduling constraints.

IV.

I Ching 易經

Understanding Change and the Dynamics of a Situation

Remote-friendly

Some questions are not about you. They are about a situation. A decision you are sitting with. A relationship that has become complicated. A moment where the data in your chart is not enough.

That is where I Ching comes in.

Its 64 hexagrams map conditions, relationships, and processes of change. A reading examines the specific question you bring: what is present, what is moving underneath it, and what different courses of action are likely to produce.

Kesler draws on it when the consultation calls for it, alongside Bazi or Feng Shui.
