✦ MEET THE FOUNDER

Hi, I’m Chelle. 🌿

For years I lived the corporate script — performing, overdelivering, and quietly shrinking to fit rooms that were never built for my whole self. Burnout became my baseline.

Then came an unexpected layoff, and soon after, a mental health diagnosis. Together they stripped the script away completely. What was left was a question I had never really asked: who am I without it?

The answer didn’t come from dramatic prophecies. It came from learning to listen inward — through the cards, through my body, through stillness — and slowly, gently, learning to unshrink.

Indakdiwa — from indak (dance) and diwa (spirit) — is the tahanan I needed back then. Now it’s here for you.
