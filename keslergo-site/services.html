I.

Bazi Natal Chart Reading 八字

Understand Your Personal Chart and Life Patterns

In person or remote

Overview

Bazi, also known as the Four Pillars of Destiny, is a traditional Chinese system that examines an individual's natal chart based on their birth information.

The chart provides a framework for understanding personal characteristics, natural tendencies, strengths, challenges, and the elemental relationships that influence different areas of life.

A Bazi reading begins with an examination of the Day Master, the Five Elements, the Ten Gods, and the overall structure of the chart. The interactions between the natal pillars provide further insight into personal patterns and circumstances.

An important part of the consultation is the analysis of the current 10-year Luck Pillar. While the natal chart represents the conditions present at birth, successive Luck Pillars introduce changing influences throughout life. Their interactions with the natal chart help examine how different periods may present opportunities, challenges, and shifting circumstances.

What Can a Bazi Reading Help You Understand?

A personalized Bazi consultation may explore:

  • Personal characteristics: Your natural tendencies, strengths, challenges, and ways of approaching different situations.
  • Career and professional life: Patterns associated with work, responsibility, ambition, professional development, and career direction.
  • Finances: Your relationship with wealth, financial opportunities, and potential challenges reflected in the chart.
  • Relationships: Personal interaction patterns, relationship dynamics, and relevant influences within the natal chart.
  • Life cycles: How changing Luck Pillars interact with your natal chart and what these influences may suggest about different periods.

A Bazi reading is relevant both to individuals seeking greater self-understanding and those examining specific difficulties, changes, or decisions in their lives.

What to Expect

Each consultation is individually prepared and conducted one-on-one with Kesler.

The session includes a detailed explanation of the natal chart, its major characteristics and interactions, and the current 10-year Luck Pillar. Clients have the opportunity to ask questions and discuss the interpretation in relation to their circumstances.

Copies of the relevant Bazi charts and presentation slides are provided for reference.

Consultation Arrangements

Sessions are primarily conducted through Zoom. In-person consultations may be arranged in Makati upon request.

Information Required

Date, time, and place of birth. A reading may still be conducted without an exact birth time, although some aspects of the interpretation will be less precise.

II.

Feng Shui 風水

Understanding the Environment Around You

In-person only (Metro Manila). Never virtual.

Overview

Feng Shui is the traditional Chinese practice of examining the relationship between a physical environment, its orientation, surrounding landforms, and the influences associated with how a space is configured and used.

A Feng Shui assessment considers the property as a whole. Its location, external surroundings, compass orientation, architectural layout, entrances, room arrangements, and other physical characteristics all contribute to the analysis.

Different properties may respond differently to the same general recommendations. For this reason, an assessment must consider the actual conditions of the location rather than rely on generic Feng Shui advice.

What Does a
Feng Shui Audit Examine?

Kesler conducts on-site assessments using relevant traditional Feng Shui methods, compass measurements, floor plans, and observations of the surrounding environment. The assessment considers several interconnected factors:

  • External environment: Roads, neighboring structures, landforms, and other features surrounding the property.
  • Property orientation: Compass measurements and the relevant directional relationships used in Feng Shui calculations.
  • Internal configuration: Entrances, room placements, circulation, functional areas, and the overall arrangement of the space.
  • Feng Shui influences: Relevant traditional calculations used to examine favorable and challenging conditions within the property.
  • Practical considerations: Existing architectural constraints, furniture arrangements, intended uses, and the feasibility of recommended adjustments.

The objective is to develop an informed understanding of the property's Feng Shui characteristics and identify appropriate recommendations within the realities of the space.

When Is a
Feng Shui Assessment Relevant?

Feng Shui consultations may be considered for residential homes, condominium units, offices, commercial establishments, and other properties.

Common reasons for requesting an assessment include purchasing or moving into a property, planning renovations, establishing a new office, relocating a business, or reviewing the Feng Shui of an existing space.

An audit can also provide a more systematic understanding of a property's strengths and challenges before significant changes are made.

What to Expect

Feng Shui audits are conducted in person.

During the site visit, Kesler examines the property's physical conditions, takes relevant compass measurements, reviews its configuration, and evaluates the environmental features necessary for the assessment.

Floor plans and photographs support the analysis, but they cannot fully replace an inspection of the actual property.

Following the assessment, clients receive a written Feng Shui report containing the relevant findings and recommendations.

Recommendations take into account the property's existing conditions and practical limitations. They may involve the use or arrangement of particular spaces and other adjustments appropriate to the assessment.

Assessment Arrangements

Available within Metro Manila and surrounding areas. Other locations may be accommodated by prior arrangement, subject to logistics.

Information Required

Property address, available floor plans or layout sketches, and relevant information about the property's intended use.

III.

Date Selection 擇日

Choosing Suitable Timing for Important Activities

Remote-friendly

Overview

Date Selection is the traditional Chinese practice of evaluating dates and times to identify suitable periods for particular activities.

In Chinese metaphysics, timing is examined in relation to the nature of an activity, the characteristics of the selected date, and the individuals involved.

A date that is considered suitable for one activity may not necessarily be appropriate for another. Date Selection therefore requires more than simply choosing a generally auspicious day from the Chinese calendar.

How Date Selection Works

Kesler evaluates potential dates using established Chinese calendrical methods and relevant personal information.

The assessment considers the purpose of the event, applicable traditional date-selection principles, relevant interactions involving the individuals concerned, and any practical restrictions on scheduling.

The objective is to identify suitable options within the available timeframe while avoiding unfavorable interactions where appropriate.

Practical circumstances are an important part of the process. Dates must be evaluated in the context of venue availability, business requirements, family arrangements, and other relevant constraints.

When Is Date Selection Relevant?

Date Selection may be requested for important personal, family, and business activities, including:

  • Weddings and significant family occasions
  • Business openings and launches
  • Office relocations and residential move-ins
  • Groundbreaking and construction-related ceremonies
  • Contract signings and other important business activities
  • Other events where suitable traditional timing is desired
What to Expect

Each Date Selection engagement is prepared according to the specific activity and the circumstances of those involved.

Clients receive a written Date Selection report identifying recommended dates and, where relevant, suitable time periods.

The recommendations reflect traditional date-selection principles and the practical parameters established for the event. A selected date does not guarantee any particular outcome.

Information Required

The nature of the event, preferred scheduling window, relevant birth information of the principal individuals involved, and any important practical restrictions.

IV.

I Ching 易經

Understanding Change and the Dynamics of a Situation

Remote-friendly

Overview

The I Ching, or Book of Changes, is one of the foundational texts of Chinese philosophical and divinatory tradition.

Its system of 64 hexagrams represents different conditions, relationships, and processes of change. Through the interpretation of hexagrams and their changing lines, I Ching divination examines the dynamics of a particular question or situation.

Where Bazi examines an individual's natal chart and changing life cycles, I Ching approaches a question through the specific conditions represented by the resulting hexagram.

An interpretation may consider the present situation, underlying influences, possible developments, and the implications of different responses or courses of action.

I Ching Within Kesler's Practice

Kesler incorporates I Ching as a supporting method within his broader work in Chinese metaphysics.

When relevant, I Ching may provide an additional interpretive perspective alongside Bazi or Feng Shui, particularly when a particular circumstance calls for closer examination.

Its application depends on the question and the nature of the consultation.

I Ching remains an important part of Kesler's professional practice and technical knowledge, although it is not offered as a separately bookable consultation.
