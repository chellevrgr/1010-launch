The Practice

Chinese metaphysics encompasses traditional systems for understanding people, their environments, and the influence of timing.
These methods examine personal characteristics, changing circumstances, environmental influences, and potential future developments.

Kesler practices Bazi to interpret individual natal charts and life cycles, Feng Shui to assess homes and workplaces, and Date Selection to identify suitable timing for important activities. I Ching also forms part of his practice, providing additional perspectives when applied alongside Bazi or Feng Shui.

Grounded in traditional Chinese knowledge and developed through formal training, continued study, and practical experience, his approach emphasizes careful analysis, personal interpretation, and clear explanations.

Whether seeking greater self-understanding, examining current challenges, or preparing for important changes, clients receive considered interpretations that make complex metaphysical principles understandable and relevant to their circumstances.
